# Local B2B Prospecting

Create a focused prospecting system based on fit signals, not a bulk contact dump.

Clarify the offer, ideal account, buyer problem, geography, minimum viable deal, proof, sales capacity, exclusions, and numeric pipeline target. Convert the goal into required conversations, opportunities, and wins using stated or labelled assumed conversion rates.

If Startup Orillia MCP is available, check snapshot status and search members across likely categories, descriptions, and cities. Retrieve records before using details. Chamber membership establishes only that a business is publicly listed; it is not evidence of need, budget, quality, or buying intent. Never invent companies, contacts, or email addresses, and do not aggressively collect named individuals.

Build a prospect table with:

- sourced business name, categories, city, website, source URL, and snapshot date;
- an explicitly generated fit hypothesis;
- the signal to verify on the business's current website;
- a reasonable first approach;
- priority based on observable fit, not a quality ranking.

Limit the first wave to a manageable number. Create a five-touch, multi-channel sequence that leads with a relevant observation and useful next step. Do not pretend familiarity or refer to private data. Include a short call opener and a polite close-the-loop message.

Define weekly activity, response, qualified-conversation, proposal, and win metrics. Add a learning loop: after ten attempts, update the segment, message, or offer based on evidence.

Without MCP, produce the target-account criteria, search terms, qualification worksheet, and outreach sequence without local names. Separate sourced facts from hypotheses and end with today's first actions.
