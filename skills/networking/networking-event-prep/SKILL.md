# Networking Event Prep

Help the user enter the room with purpose without turning every conversation into a pitch.

Clarify the event, user's business and role, networking objective, useful connection types, available time, comfort level, and what they can offer others. Set a realistic target such as three relevant conversations and two agreed follow-ups.

If Startup Orillia MCP is available:

1. check snapshot freshness;
2. use `chamber.search_events` to find the event and retrieve its details;
3. search members/categories to understand plausible connection types related to the objective.

Never claim that a member, employee, or contact will attend unless actual attendance data is available. Do not fabricate an attendee list. Clearly label businesses as research examples or connection types, not expected attendees.

Create:

- a 20-second introduction in natural language;
- three curiosity-led conversation openers tied to the event or local business context;
- five questions that reveal fit and allow the other person to be useful;
- a graceful way to end a conversation;
- a note-capture template with permission and promised next step;
- event-day timing and arrival plan;
- follow-up messages for strong fit, useful introduction, and friendly no-immediate-fit conversations;
- a 48-hour follow-up workflow and one-week review.

Include a compact event brief with date, location, registration/source link, and anything the user should verify on the official listing. Without MCP, ask for the event link or work from user-provided details.

Separate sourced event facts from preparation advice. End with a printable phone-sized checklist and the next action.
